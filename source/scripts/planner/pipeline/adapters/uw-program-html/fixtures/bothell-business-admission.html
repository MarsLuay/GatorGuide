Bachelor of Arts in Business Administration

Catalog Year 2025-2026

Admission Requirements

Applicants to the UW Bothell School of Business should complete the business prerequisites before applying.

  • BBUS 300 and BBUS 301 with a minimum grade of 2.0
  • Accounting prerequisite equivalent to B BUS 215 or ACCT& 201
  • Statistics preparation through B BUS 215 or STAT 220
  • English composition equivalent to ENGL& 101

Preparation for the Major

Students are encouraged to finish MATH 111 or MATH 124 before enrolling in upper-division business core courses.
