# Focus GatorGuide on Green River-to-UW transfer planning

GatorGuide will be a transfer-planning product for Green River College students targeting undergraduate programs at UW Seattle, UW Bothell, or UW Tacoma. The product centers one Living Transfer Plan through transfer and removes conversational/generative AI, college ranking, Saved Colleges, College Comparison, Cost Calculator, and the separate Home surface so broad college discovery cannot compete with planner correctness.
