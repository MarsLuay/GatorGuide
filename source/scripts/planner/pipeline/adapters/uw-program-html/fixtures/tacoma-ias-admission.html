Bachelor of Arts in Interdisciplinary Arts and Sciences

Catalog Year 2025-2026

Admission Requirements

Students applying to UW Tacoma Interdisciplinary Arts and Sciences should show college-level writing, quantitative reasoning, and IAS preparation.

  • TIAS 305: Foundations of Interdisciplinary Inquiry
  • TIAS 340 or TIAS 360 for inquiry and methods preparation
  • TWRT 211 or ENGL& 101 for writing preparation
  • TMATH 110, TMATH 120, or MATH& 146 for quantitative skills

Major Preparation

Completion of TIAS 305 is recommended before upper-division IAS coursework.
